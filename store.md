Murmur shows new Discord messages from the channels you choose as a quiet, plain-text feed on your G2.

**On your phone**
- Sign in with your Discord account (token, or email and password with 2FA codes).
- Pick servers and channels to watch: tick channels one by one or switch on a whole server, and optionally include direct messages.
- Respects channels, categories, servers and DMs you've muted in Discord.
- Choose how long messages stay on the glasses before fading out.

**On your glasses**
- Each message appears as "server :: channel :: user" with the message below, newest at the bottom, and fades out after your timeout.
- Links and media show as short tags like [image], [gif] or [link].
- Swipe up to look back through earlier messages; tap or wait 30 seconds to return to live.
- Double-tap for the menu: filter the feed to one channel, show all channels again, pause and resume, or exit.
- A corner marker shows the state: ● connected, ○ not connected, ■ paused, ▲ history.

**Battery and data**
Discord traffic is compressed and limited to what Murmur needs, and Murmur stops drawing (and after a delay you set, disconnects) when the glasses are in their case or disconnected.

**Privacy**
Murmur talks only to Discord. Your sign-in is stored on your phone and never sent anywhere else. Nothing is posted to Discord on your behalf; Murmur only reads.

**Please note:** Murmur signs in as your own Discord user account. Discord's Terms of Service don't allow third-party clients for user accounts, so using Murmur may put your Discord account at risk. Murmur is not made by or affiliated with Discord.
